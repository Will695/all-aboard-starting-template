# UGC-style showcase videos: scripts and prompts

Three short, phone-shot style videos of a new hire using the app. Each is 20 to 30 seconds, vertical (9:16), one person talking to camera with the app shown on screen.

These are scripts and generation prompts, not finished videos. Paste each prompt into an AI video tool (for example Veo, Sora, Runway or HeyGen), then cut in screen recordings of the real app.

## How to make each one

1. Generate the talking clips from the "Video prompt" below. Generate each line as its own 5 to 8 second clip, so the lip sync stays clean.
2. Record the app screens listed under "Screens to cut in". Use a phone-sized browser window (375 x 812), and the demo buttons to reach each state.
3. Edit: talking clip, then screen recording with the voice continuing over it, then back to the person for the last line.
4. Add captions. Most people watch these with the sound off.
5. Label the video "AI-generated demo". The person is synthetic and is not a real Lloyds colleague.

Keep the same character in all three videos. Use the same description each time, and the same reference image if your tool supports one.

**Character:** a new hire in their late twenties, casual smart clothes, warm and a little nervous, speaking naturally to a phone camera. Home setting: a kitchen or living room in daylight, slightly handheld framing.

## Video 1: "I got the job. Now what?" (the warm handover)

**Length:** about 25 seconds. **Feeds:** prompts 01 and 03.

| Time | On screen | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4s | Person, excited, phone in hand | "Okay, I got the job at Lloyds. And I was dreading the paperwork." |
| 4 to 10s | Screen: welcome and sign-in, then the dashboard tour | "But they sent me this. No new password, it just knew who I was, and it walked me round." |
| 10 to 18s | Screen: Home, "Your journey" and "What is unlocked" | "It shows exactly where I am, and what I can do right now. First job: sign my contract." |
| 18 to 25s | Person, relaxed | "I have never known this much before a first day. I'm actually looking forward to it." |

**Screens to cut in:** sign-in page, the tour (Home, "Take the tour"), Home journey bar, "What is unlocked" panel.

**Video prompt:**
> Vertical 9:16 phone-shot video, handheld selfie framing. A new hire in their late twenties in casual smart clothes sits at a kitchen table in soft daylight, holding a phone, speaking warmly and a little excitedly straight to camera. Natural, unscripted UGC style, slight camera movement, no text on screen, no logos. They say: "Okay, I got the job at Lloyds. And I was dreading the paperwork."

Repeat the prompt for the last line, changing the mood to "relaxed and smiling".

## Before you publish

- The app uses the Lloyds logo. Check with the brand team before any of this goes outside the hackathon.
- Keep the generated person free of logos and real brand marks. Put the branding in the screen recordings only.
- Everything in the app is sample data. Alex, Jordan and Sam are not real people.
